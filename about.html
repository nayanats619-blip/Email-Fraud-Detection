Detection Indicators

🚫 High Risk Signals

  • ⚠️ Phishing keywords
  • ⚠️ Urgency language & threats
  • ⚠️ Requests for sensitive data
  • ⚠️ Suspicious domains
  • ⚠️ Suspicious action buttons

⚠️ Moderate Risk Signals

  • ⚡ Unusual formatting
  • ⚡ Multiple capitalization
  • ⚡ Generic greetings
  • ⚡ Vague information
  • ⚡ Multiple calls to action
📊

Understanding Risk Scores

✅
0-30% - SAFE

Email appears legitimate with no major red flags. Safe to open and read.

⚠️
30-70% - SUSPICIOUS

Email contains warning signs. Review carefully before clicking links or providing information.

🚨
70-100% - FRAUD

High probability of fraud. Do not click links or provide any information. Delete immediately.
